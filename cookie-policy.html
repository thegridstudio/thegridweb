Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 2026  |  thegrid.es

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to remember your preferences and understand how you interact with it. Cookies may be set by the site you are visiting (first-party) or by third-party services.

2. Cookies We Use

The following table lists every cookie set or read by thegrid.es:

Cookie name Provider Purpose Type Consent required Duration
tgrid_consent thegrid.es Stores your cookie consent choice (accept/reject analytics) Necessary No 12 months
_ga Google Analytics 4 Distinguishes unique users by assigning a randomly generated number as client identifier Analytics Yes 2 years
_ga_DT00P1WJ1F Google Analytics 4 Stores and counts page views and session state for this specific GA4 property Analytics Yes 2 years
_gid Google Analytics 4 Registers a unique ID to generate statistical data on how the visitor uses the site (expires daily) Analytics Yes 24 hours

Note: Analytics cookies (_ga, _ga_DT00P1WJ1F, _gid) are only set if you accept analytics cookies. If you reject them, Google Analytics still loads but operates in cookieless mode — no cookies are stored on your device and no persistent identifier is used.

3. How We Use Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 with Consent Mode v2. This means:

  • When you accept analytics cookies, GA sets the above cookies and tracks your session normally.
  • When you reject analytics cookies, GA loads without cookies and uses behavioural modelling to produce aggregate, anonymised traffic estimates. No personal data is stored on your device.

We have also configured GA4 with IP anonymisation and disabled Google Signals and advertising features. We do not pass any personally identifiable information to Google Analytics.

4. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can change your cookie preferences at any time by clicking the Cookie settings link in the footer of any page on this site. This will reopen the cookie preference panel, where you can accept or reject analytics cookies.

You can also control cookies directly through your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

To opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites, you can also install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

5. More Information

For more details on how we handle your personal data, please read our Privacy Policy. If you have any questions about our use of cookies, contact us at info@thegrid.es.
