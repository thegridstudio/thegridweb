2. Data We Collect and Why

We process personal data for the following purposes:

  • Navigation and analytics data — collected via Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site (pages visited, session duration, traffic sources). This data is anonymous or pseudonymous. Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6.1.a).
  • Contact form submissions — name and email address provided when you fill in our contact form. Used solely to respond to your enquiry. Legal basis: your consent / legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6.1.a / 6.1.f).
  • Account and service data on dashboard.thegrid.es — data required to provide access to the O-SAM structural engineering platform (email, authentication credentials, uploaded models and data). Legal basis: performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6.1.b).

We do not collect sensitive personal data (GDPR Art. 9) and we do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal effects.

3. No Personal Data Sent to Google Analytics

We have configured Google Analytics 4 to:

  • Anonymise IP addresses before any data is stored (anonymize_ip: true).
  • Disable Google Signals and cross-device tracking (allow_google_signals: false).
  • Disable advertising personalisation signals (allow_ad_personalization_signals: false).

We do not pass any user identifiers, email addresses, or other PII to Google Analytics.

4. Data Retention

  • Analytics data: retained for a maximum of 25 months in Google Analytics (as required by the AEPD).
  • Contact form data: retained for the time necessary to respond to your enquiry and for a maximum of 2 years thereafter.
  • Consent records (tgrid_consent cookie): stored locally in your browser for 12 months.
  • Account data on dashboard.thegrid.es: retained for the duration of the service relationship and for the legally required period after termination.

6. Your Rights

Under the GDPR and the Spanish Organic Law on Data Protection (LOPDGDD), you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — obtain confirmation of whether we process your data and receive a copy.
  • Rectification — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten") — request deletion of your data when it is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected.
  • Restriction of processing — request that we limit how we use your data in certain circumstances.
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Withdraw consent — withdraw any consent you have given at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. To withdraw analytics consent, use the Cookie settings link in the footer.

7. How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of the above rights, please send a written request to info@thegrid.es with the subject line "Data Subject Request". We will respond within 30 calendar days.

8. Right to Lodge a Complaint

If you believe your data protection rights have been violated, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Authority (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos — AEPD): www.aepd.es.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our data practices or applicable law. The date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised. Continued use of the site after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
